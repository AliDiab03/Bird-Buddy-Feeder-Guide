Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026  ·  Applies to the Feeder Companion Android app

Feeder Companion ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent, unofficial setup and troubleshooting guide for the Bird Buddy smart feeder. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Bird Buddy. This policy explains what information the app interacts with, why, and what choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not collect your name, email address, precise location, photos, postcards, or any content from your Bird Buddy feeder or its official companion app. All guide content (setup steps, feature explanations, specs, FAQs, retailer links) is stored locally in the app and does not require sending your personal data to us. This app never connects to your feeder, your Bird Buddy account, or your postcards — it is a standalone reference only.

2. Advertising & third-party services

The app is supported by ads served through Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, and app-open ad formats. To serve and measure these ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process technical data such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • General, approximate location derived from IP address
  • App usage and interaction data related to ad performance

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy, not directly by us. We do not receive or store this data ourselves. You can review how Google handles this information at policies.google.com/privacy, and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

3. Links to third-party retailers

The "Where to Buy" section links out to third-party retailer websites (such as Amazon, the official Bird Buddy store, and Target) so you can compare prices. Once you follow one of these links, that retailer's own privacy policy applies — we have no control over, and are not responsible for, how they handle your data.

4. Analytics

We may use basic, aggregated analytics (such as app opens or crash reports) to understand how the app is used and to fix bugs. This data is anonymized or pseudonymized and is not used to identify you personally.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Data retention & security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is minimal data for us to retain. Any data processed by third-party ad or analytics providers is retained according to their respective policies.

7. Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or via Google Ads Settings).
  • Reset or limit your Advertising ID from your Android device settings at any time.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection related to it.

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA regarding data processed by our third-party providers, which can typically be exercised directly with Google.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. The "effective date" above will always reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
