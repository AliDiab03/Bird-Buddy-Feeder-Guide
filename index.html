Paired up in five steps

The exact sequence the guide walks you through — from an empty box to your first postcard.

01

Charge the camera first

Fully charge the camera module before pairing — an undercharged unit is a common cause of a stalled setup.

02

Create your account & start pairing

Open the app, sign up, and choose "Start with a new Birdbuddy." Allow Bluetooth & Location when prompted.

03

Insert the camera & wait for the chirp

Snap the module into the feeder until it clicks and the alignment lines are straight.

04

Connect to your 2.4GHz WiFi

2.4GHz is more reliable outdoors than 5GHz, even on models that support both.

05

Let the firmware update finish

Keep it powered and nearby until this completes — then your first postcard is on its way.

Answers to the questions people actually ask

A preview of what's covered in the troubleshooting section.

App can't find the camera while pairing

Reinsert the camera module until it clicks, keep your phone close, and confirm Bluetooth + Location are allowed.

WiFi step won't connect

Move the feeder closer to your router for setup, and connect to a 2.4GHz network specifically.

Blinking red or orange light

Red usually means low charge. Orange/yellow can signal a firmware issue — contact Bird Buddy support if it persists.

Missing postcards

Check the feeder's WiFi signal strength and make sure app notifications are turned on.
